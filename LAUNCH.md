# Launch and distribution

Live since 2026-10-25 at https://theguysaccount.github.io/lifeclock/. Machine channels only: nothing is posted by or as a person.

## Channels that run themselves

| Channel | How |
|---|---|
| Search | Home ("life clock", "how many seconds do I have left", "death clock", "life expectancy calculator"), billion-second calculator ("when will I be a billion seconds old"), 186 life-expectancy pages ("life expectancy in Japan by age", "average years left at 40"), each with its own title, description, canonical and structured data (WebApplication, Dataset). |
| IndexNow | Every deploy submits all URLs. |
| Share loop | The share card (number only, site address at the bottom) for Instagram stories, X and messages; native share sheet on phones. The billion-second card is the positive, birthday-style variant. |
| Desk mode | A clock left running on a second screen is seen by everyone who walks past. |
| GitHub | Public repo with description, topics and README. |

## The first 1,000 users (machine plan)

1. **Week 1:** IndexNow covers Bing-family engines. Google needs Search Console (OPERATIONS.md key 4).
2. **Ongoing:** country pages pick up long-tail searches; the billion-second page catches milestone-birthday searches.
3. **Share cards:** the measurable loop once analytics are on (key 3). Target: 20% of clock starts make a card.

Targets at 14 days, once analytics are on: 1,000 clock starts, 20% making a card, 5% opening desk mode.
